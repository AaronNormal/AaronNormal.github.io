Getting started

What Everl is and how it works.

What is Everl?

Everl is a free app for meeting people near you in person. Switch on when you’re free, pick what you’re up for, and when someone nearby wants the same and you both say yes, you meet where you both choose. Live in Manchester.

What’s the difference between Social and Something more (18+)?

Social is for plans and friends: a drink, an activity, or just company. Something more is the side for adult dating, and you choose to switch it on. It’s the same app, one switch away, and it isn’t behind a paywall.

Everyone is 18+. The first time you switch on, an independent checker confirms your age with a quick selfie. Everl only gets a yes or no. One Everl+ works on both sides.

How do meets work?

Switch on when you’re free and pick what you’re up for. Everl tells you when someone nearby is up for it, without showing anyone a profile. When you both say yes, You agree where to meet and Everl guides you both there.

There are no profiles and no chat. You’re anonymous until it’s mutual, and you meet in the open, on your terms.

Do I need to verify my age?

Yes. Everyone is 18+. The first time you switch on, an independent checker confirms your age with a quick selfie. Everl only gets a yes or no.

It happens once, and it’s the same for both sides.

How does the waitlist work?

Manchester is live. Leeds opens next. Lots of other city lists are open, or you can start your own. Join your city’s list with your email, then confirm from the link we email you. We’ll email you when Everl opens there.

We only email you about your city and the odd update, and you can leave any time from the link at the bottom of our emails.

How do invites work?

Everyone on a city list gets a personal invite link. When friends join through it, you move up your city’s list.

Invites are anonymous: your friend never sees who invited them, and you only see how many people joined, never who.

What do founding members get?

Founding members are the first people on the list in each city. As a thank-you, they get a code for their first 3 months of Everl+ free.

The code works on the monthly or annual plan, not the 30-day pass.

Which cities are live, and when does mine open?

Manchester is live. Leeds opens next. Lots of other city lists are open, or you can start your own. Join yours on the Cities page and we’ll email you when it opens.

How does Everl find people near me?

Switch on when you’re free and Everl tells you when someone nearby is up for it.

Your exact location isn’t shared with other people. If you use the safety alert, your safety contact gets your location.

Does Everl show how far away people are?

No. Everl never shows where anyone is or how far away they are. You see how many people nearby are up for the same thing.

Once you’ve both said yes, You agree where to meet and Everl guides you both there.

How do you know the other person is actually there?

As you get close, Everl can use both phones’ Bluetooth to check you’re in the same place.

You never see where the other person is, and they never see where you are.

What actually happens in a meet?

When you and someone nearby both say yes, You agree where to meet and Everl guides you both there.

There are no profiles and no chat beforehand: the point is to meet in person, low-pressure.

At any point you can leave, block, or use the safety alert. You’re always in charge.

What is a knock, and will I get notified?

If someone was up for meeting nearby while you had the app closed, you can get a discreet nudge, a knock. It carries no identity and no message, just that the moment was there.

Notifications are optional and always discreet. You choose whether to turn them on.

Membership and billing

Everl+, the free trial, changing or cancelling.

What does Everl+ cost?

Everl is free. Everl+ is optional: £17.99 a month, £179 a year or £24 for 30 days. Cancel any time.

Meeting someone on Everl is free: joining, matching and meeting in person cost nothing, one meet at a time. Everl+ lets you line up more than one, plan ahead and see the best times to go.

Annual is £179 a year, about £14.92 a month, billed once a year. 3 days free. Monthly also starts with 3 days free. The 30-day pass is paid once and doesn’t renew.

How does the free trial work?

The trial is 3 days and costs £0. If you don’t cancel before the 3 days are up, it rolls into the plan you chose and the first charge is taken then. We email you the exact date when your trial starts.

Cancel any time during the trial and you won’t be charged. There’s one free trial per person, and the 30-day pass has no trial.

How do I cancel Everl+?

If you paid on everl.com, log in at everl.com/account, choose Cancel Everl+ and confirm. If you subscribed in the app, cancel in your Apple or Google subscription settings. You keep Everl+ until the end of the period you’ve already paid for, and there are no further charges.

If you pay in the app, Apple or Google bill you, so cancel in your subscriptions on the App Store or Google Play.

How do I change my plan?

In your account you can switch between monthly and annual. Upgrading charges only the difference for the time left in your current period, and downgrading credits the unused time. The exact amounts show on your receipt.

Can I get a refund?

If you pay on everl.com, you can cancel within 14 days of starting for a refund of anything you haven’t used. After that, cancelling stops future charges and you keep Everl+ until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

If something’s gone wrong, like a duplicate charge or a charge you don’t recognise, message us with the date and we’ll put it right. If you pay in the app, refunds go through Apple or Google. Your rights as a UK consumer always apply.

What will show on my bank statement?

If you pay on everl.com, your statement shows HPR SYSTM, not Everl. If you pay in the app, Apple or Google bill you and their receipt names the app.

Safety and privacy

How we keep you anonymous and safe.

Is Everl anonymous and discreet?

Yes. You’re anonymous until it’s mutual: no profile, no photo, no name. Nobody knows who you are unless you both say yes.

We keep as little as possible. If you pay on everl.com, your statement shows HPR SYSTM, not Everl.

What data do you keep?

As little as possible. Matching uses your approximate area. During a meet, your phone uses precise location and Bluetooth to help you find each other, and your exact location isn’t shared with other people. There are no profiles and no chat.

What we do hold: your email and city; your membership status (card details stay with our payment provider, Stripe, never with us); and any support messages you send us. Payment records are kept as UK tax law requires.

You can delete your account and data any time from your account. Full detail is in our Privacy policy.

How does the safety alert work?

During a meet you can use the safety alert at any time. It sends a real message (a text, WhatsApp or call) to a safety contact you’ve saved, with your location. Save a safety contact in your account so it’s ready.

If you ever feel unsafe, put your safety first and call emergency services (999 in the UK).

How do I report or block someone?

You can report and block from within or after a meet. A block works straight away, and you and that person will never be matched again. Reports go to a real person; we aim to review them within 24 hours, and anything urgent is escalated immediately. Serious or repeated reports lead to account action.

If someone is in immediate danger, call emergency services first (999 in the UK).

Do you check who people are?

Everyone is 18+. The first time you switch on, an independent checker confirms your age with a quick selfie. Everl only gets a yes or no. We don’t run background checks, and we hold no profiles or real names.

Safety comes from how meets work instead: they’re in person, brief, and on your terms. You can block or use the safety alert at any moment, and reports go to a real team that tracks repeat offenders. If you ever feel unsafe, put yourself first and call emergency services (999 in the UK).

Is Everl safe for women?

You’re in control from the start. There’s no profile or photo for anyone to browse, and nobody knows who you are until you both say yes.

You both agree where to meet, somewhere that feels safe to you. You can leave, block or report at any point, with no explanation owed, and your safety contact is one hold away during a meet. Everyone is 18+ and age-checked. If you’re ever in danger, call 999 first.

Your account

Recovery, deletion, and email issues.

How do I recover my account?

Use the recovery option and enter the email you signed up with: we’ll send a link to restore access. If you’re stuck, contact support with the email address you used and we’ll help you back in.

How do I delete my account and data?

In your account, choose Delete account and all data, and confirm. This cancels an Everl+ subscription you pay for on everl.com and removes your membership and data. It can’t be undone.

If you subscribe in the app, also cancel in your subscriptions on the App Store or Google Play, because Apple or Google bill you there. We keep payment records only as long as UK tax law requires.

I didn’t get my confirmation email

First check your spam or promotions folder, as a new sender can land there. We only send the confirmation on a new signup: if that email address already joined, we don’t send it again, so you may already be on the list.

If a brand-new address still gets nothing after a few minutes, contact support and we’ll look into it.

We aim to reply to every message within 24 hours. Anything about your safety is answered first and never waits in a queue.
