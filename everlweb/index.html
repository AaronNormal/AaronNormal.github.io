FAQs

Questions, answered.

Is Everl for dating or for friends?

Everl starts on Social, for plans and new friends. Something more is a separate side for meeting someone, and you choose it yourself.

Is Everl for straight people?

Everl is for everyone over 18, whoever you are and whoever you’re into. On Something more you say who you’re looking for, in private.

Will there be anyone near me?

Everl is live in Manchester now, and Leeds is next. When you switch on, you see how many people nearby are up for the same thing. If nobody is yet, stay on and Everl tells you when someone is.

How old do I need to be?

Everl is for over 18s only. The first time you switch on, an independent checker confirms your age with a quick selfie. Everl only gets a yes or no.

Do I need a profile or a photo?

No. There are no profiles or photos, and no chat before you meet. You pick what you’re up for and Everl does the rest.

What if it’s awkward?

You meet somewhere you both agree on, to do one thing you both chose. If it isn’t for you, you can leave. The other person is never told why.

Who can see where I am?

Nobody can see where you are. Others only see how many people nearby are up for something. Where you meet is only shared once you’ve both said yes.

Does Everl show how far away people are?

No. Everl never shows where anyone is or how far away they are. You see how many people nearby are up for the same thing, and you decide how far you’re happy to go. Once you’ve both said yes, you agree where to meet and Everl guides you both there.

Is it free?

Yes, Everl is free. Everl+ is optional: £17.99 a month, £179 a year or £24 for 30 days. Monthly and yearly start with 3 days free. Cancel any time.
