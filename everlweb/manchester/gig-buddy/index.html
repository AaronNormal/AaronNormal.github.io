FAQs

Is Everl for dating or for friends?

Everl starts on Social, for plans and new friends. Up for more is a separate side for meeting someone, and you choose it yourself.

Will there be anyone near me?

Everl is live in Manchester now, and Leeds is next. When you switch on, you see how many people nearby are up for the same thing. If nobody is yet, stay on and Everl tells you when someone is.

How old do I need to be?

Everl is for over 18s only. Everyone does a quick, one-off age check by an independent verifier before they first switch on. Everl only gets a yes or no.

Do I need a profile or a photo?

No. There are no profiles or photos, and no chat before you meet. You pick what you're up for and Everl does the rest.

What if it's awkward?

You meet somewhere busy and public, to do one thing you both chose. If it isn't for you, you can leave. The other person is never told why.

Who can see where I am?

Nobody can see you on a map. Others only see how many people nearby are up for something. The meeting place is only shared once you've both said yes.

Is it free?

Yes, Everl is free. Everl+ is optional: £17.99 a month, or £179 a year (£14.99 a month), or a one-off £24 pass for 30 days. Monthly and yearly start with 3 days free, and you can cancel any time.
