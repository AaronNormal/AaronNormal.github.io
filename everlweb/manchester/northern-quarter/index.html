The Northern Quarter

The busiest few streets in town.

The Northern Quarter has more independent bars, cafés and small venues per street than anywhere else in Manchester. On a Friday you can’t move on Thomas Street.

That density makes it a good place to meet someone new. Switch on, pick a coffee, a gig or a drink, and when someone wants the same you’ll both know. Everl picks a public place between you, and in the Northern Quarter it’s never far.

It works in the day too. Record shops, vintage shops and cafés make it easy to fill a Saturday with someone new. Pick a coffee and see where it goes.

  • Coffee by day, gigs and bars by night
  • Plenty of busy public places
  • Easy walk from Piccadilly and Victoria

Getting there

The Northern Quarter sits between Piccadilly Gardens and Ancoats. Stevenson Square, with its painted walls, is one of the easiest places in town to find each other. Afflecks and Piccadilly Gardens are the other easy landmarks if you’re coming from the station.

Questions

Will Everl tell people I’m in the Northern Quarter?

No. Nobody can see you on a map, and Everl never shows anyone’s neighbourhood. The meeting place is only shared once you’ve both said yes.

Can I use Everl for friends?

Yes. Social is for plans and new friends. Up for more is a separate 18+ side for dating.

What can I do there on Everl?

Anything you’re up for: coffee, gigs, drinks, board games, a photowalk. Pick it and switch on.

Is the Northern Quarter good for a first meet?

It’s one of the best: busy, central and full of places where you can have one drink and leave, or stay all evening.
