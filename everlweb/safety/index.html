HomeSafety

Built to keep you safe, by design.

Meeting someone new should feel good, not risky. Everl's whole design starts from one question: is the most exposed person safe if someone glances over their shoulder? Here's what that means in practice.

Anonymous until you both choose

There are no profiles and no photos. You're a count and a codename until you and one other person both decide to meet. Nobody can browse you, screenshot you, or build a picture of who you are.

You meet in public

Everl only ever sends you to a real, public place (a fountain, a park gate, a busy square), never a private address, and never a pin dropped on a person. You choose to go, and you can stop at any point with no explanation owed.

A safety contact, one tap away

You can set a trusted contact in advance. If you ever feel unsafe, a discreet hold-to-alert sends them your last known location and a heads-up. It's never promoted, never pushy. It's just there if you need it, and we always say: call emergency services first if you're in danger.

Nothing lingers

Presence fades on its own. We keep the minimum and delete the rest, so there's simply very little to leak. You can delete your account and everything tied to it in a single tap, any time.

In danger right now?

Call 999 first. Then use the safety control in the app to alert your safety contact.
